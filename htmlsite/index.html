Why Play Matters

Giant pandas naturally spend hours foraging, climbing, and exploring. Our enrichment playgrounds stimulate their natural behaviors, ensuring they stay physically fit and mentally sharp.

Physical Health

Climbing structures and dynamic terrain prevent obesity and build strong muscles crucial for their well-being.

Mental Stimulation

Puzzle feeders and complex layouts challenge their intelligence, reducing stress and boredom in captivity.

Joyful Living

Simply put, playing makes them happy! A happy panda is more active and has better overall health outcomes.
