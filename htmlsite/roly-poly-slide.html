The Roly-Poly Slide

A gentle, wide slide specifically angled for panda comfort. Includes soft-landing zones and integrated puzzle feeders at the bottom to encourage repeated play and joyful tumbling.

Designed for Safe Tumbling

Our Roly-Poly Slide is engineered with a low-friction, padded surface that allows pandas to glide effortlessly without straining their joints. The wide base accommodates group play, fostering social interaction among residents.

  • Ergonomic 30-degree angle for comfortable sliding
  • Impact-absorbing landing zone with natural moss and sand
  • Built-in bamboo treat dispensers to reward play
